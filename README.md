# this is a standalone component for background of a website

## Concept

A minimalist, ultra realistic website background concept featuring a vast expanse of thick oil like black water trapped beneath a translucent, glass like glacier. Beneath the icy surface, the viscous liquid flows slowly, revealing subtle currents, shifting reflections, and mysterious creatures swimming in sync with the water's movement. The glacier exhibits realistic refraction, internal fractures, and light scattering, creating the illusion of a frozen world suspended above a living, dark ocean. Designed as a standalone visual experiment spanning eight vertically scrollable viewports, the experience focuses entirely on the background, material realism, fluid dynamics, and immersive environmental storytelling, with no text, UI, or other content.
